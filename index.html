HOW ODD STARTED

Normal dulls the senses.
We wanted something real.

ODD was born out of frustration with everyday fragrance options. You either had synthetic deodorants that faded in 20 minutes, or hyper-inflated designer perfumes imported at 4x cost with watered-down wear.

We set up shop with a simple rule: Focus on high perfume oil concentration (20–25% Eau De Parfum) and remove all unnecessary fluff. No celebrity endorsements, no gilded caps that end up in the trash.

Just three perfected everyday perfumes designed to make an unshakeable impression from morning standup to late dinners.
