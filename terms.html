5. Product Formulations, Notes & Olfactory Advisory

ODD fragrances are crafted as high-concentration Eau de Parfum (EDP) formulations. We strive to present accurate visual depictions, olfactory notes, and descriptions. However:

  • Subjective Perception & Skin Chemistry: Fine fragrance interacts dynamically with individual skin pH, hydration, climate, and application methods. Fragrance pyramid notes (top, heart, and base) represent our intended olfactory composition but may evolve uniquely on every wearer.
  • Packaging & Color Variations: Due to natural raw botanical extracts and screen display calibrations, liquid hues and bottle finishes may display minor natural variations.
  • Cosmetic Usage & Patch Testing: Fine fragrances contain denatured alcohol and aroma compounds. We recommend performing a small patch test on your inner wrist before extensive application. Avoid contact with eyes, damaged skin, or sensitive membranes. Discontinue use immediately if irritation occurs.
  • Flammability Caution: Fragrance formulations are flammable. Store your bottle in a cool, dark location away from heat, open flames, and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
