Hostinger SMTP & Studio Mail Configuration

Connect your official Hostinger mailbox (hello@oddperfume.com) via authenticated SSL SMTP to guarantee inbox delivery for all inquiries and order confirmations.

Why Authenticated SMTP is Required on Hostinger Hostinger Email (Titan Mail) strictly blocks unauthenticated PHP mail() calls because they lack SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment. Connecting via smtp.hostinger.com:465 (SSL) with your mailbox credentials ensures that every inquiry and order receipt reaches hello@oddperfume.com without getting dropped or marked as spam.
Default for Hostinger & Titan Mail: smtp.hostinger.com
Standard SSL port: 465 (or TLS 587)
