Privacy Policy

Consumer Data Protection & Transparency

1. Introduction & Data Fiduciary Identity

This Privacy Policy outlines how ODD Perfume (“ODD”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, processes, stores, protects, and discloses personal data when you visit oddperfume.com, create a customer account, acquire fine fragrances, participate in olfactory consultations, or interact with our client care team.

Operating as an independent luxury fragrance house based in India, ODD Perfume acts as the Data Fiduciary (or Data Controller) for your personal information. We operate in strict conformity with applicable Indian data protection frameworks, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a white-glove luxury experience, seamlessly fulfill orders, and maintain bespoke client care, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, salutation, personal telephone/mobile number, and email address provided during account creation, checkout, or client advisory inquiries.
  • Olfactory Consultation & Bespoke Preferences: Personal fragrance preferences, olfactory family affinities (e.g., woody, fresh citrus, smoky gourmand), consultation records with our studio specialists, wishlists, and personalized gifting instructions.
  • Order, Invoicing & Delivery Details: Exact postal delivery coordinates (flat/suite, building name, street, landmarks, city, state, and 6-digit postal PIN code), recipient identity, purchased bottle editions, batch serialization numbers, order history, and tax invoice records.
  • Payment & Transaction Details (Zero Financial Storage): Payment transaction status, gateway reference tokens, order amounts, and chosen payment method (Razorpay Online or Cash on Delivery). Please note: ODD Perfume strictly adheres to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directives and PCI-DSS standards. We NEVER store, record, or have access to raw credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, netbanking credentials, or UPI MPINs. All online payments are handled directly by RBI-authorized, Level 1 PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.
  • Client Account Credentials: Account identifiers and login credentials protected using salted one-way cryptographic hashing (never stored in plaintext), along with saved client address books.
  • Anti-Counterfeiting & Anti-Fraud Signals: Minimal transactional telemetry (IP addresses, device user-agents, checkout frequency, order velocity) utilized solely to prevent automated bot checkout abuse, unauthorized commercial hoarding, and fraudulent COD bookings.
  • Client Relations Correspondence: Email exchanges, WhatsApp client care conversations, phone consultations, and feedback records to ensure exemplary post-purchase satisfaction.
  • Technical & Session Data: Browser user-agent, operating system, network timestamps, and essential session identifiers necessary to ensure platform stability and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) defense.

3. How We Collect Your Information

Personal data is obtained strictly through legitimate and transparent avenues:

  1. Direct Client Submission: When you purchase a fragrance, subscribe to our olfactory journal, consult with our studio advisors, apply a launch code, or submit an inquiry via our contact avenues.
  2. Automated Platform Technologies: When you navigate our digital storefront, essential session tokens and technical cookies preserve your shopping Cart and protect your session.
  3. Authorized Operational Partners: Verified transaction confirmations from our RBI-licensed payment partner (Razorpay) and live transit updates from our logistics partner (Shiprocket).

4. Purposes of Data Processing

ODD Perfume processes personal information strictly for legitimate operational, service, and legal purposes:

  • Processing, compounding verification, packaging, and insured dispatch of fragrance orders from our fulfillment facility.
  • Issuing statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) commercial invoices, e-way bills, and legal corporate accounts.
  • Facilitating doorstep pan-India delivery across 25,000+ PIN codes via premier surface and air express couriers (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Shadowfax).
  • Delivering real-time dispatch updates, AWB tracking coordinates, and out-for-delivery notifications via SMS and email.
  • Delivering bespoke olfactory recommendations, private client previews, and invitations to new extrait formulations solely when you have provided explicit affirmative consent (every communication provides an instant one-click unsubscribe mechanism).
  • Upholding brand integrity and selective distribution: monitoring transaction patterns to prevent unauthorized commercial grey-market resale, counterfeit circulation, and automated stock scalping.
  • Facilitating client services, replacement claims for transit-damaged atomizers, and verified refunds.

5. Strict Non-Commercialization Guarantee

Zero Commercialization Pledge: ODD Perfume does NOT sell, rent, lease, monetize, or trade your personal information, contact coordinates, or fragrance preferences to third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or data brokers. Your trust is the foundation of our luxury house.

Information is disclosed strictly on a need-to-know basis to vetted operational partners bound by stringent contractual confidentiality obligations:

  • Logistics Providers: Shiprocket and partner couriers receive only recipient contact name, shipping address, PIN code, and phone number solely for physical parcel conveyance.
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay receives order identifiers and payment values to execute encrypted bank settlement under PCI-DSS Level 1 governance.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Data is disclosed only when strictly mandated by Indian statutory legislation, lawful court orders, or law enforcement inquiries.

6. Anti-Counterfeiting & Selective Distribution Protection

In accordance with high-perfumery standards and luxury market protection principles, ODD Perfume actively protects its clients against counterfeit imitations, unauthorized decanting, and illicit grey-market reselling:

  • Every genuine ODD bottle is assigned an individual batch code during formulation at our studio facility.
  • We monitor transaction quantities and order frequencies to detect unauthorized commercial resellers attempting to bypass our private end-consumer distribution model.
  • Personal data will never be used for aggressive algorithmic profiling; processing is strictly restricted to verifying client authenticity and preserving brand exclusivity.

7. Cookies and Technical Storage

ODD Perfume maintains a minimal digital footprint:

  • Essential Session Cookies: Mandatory cookies that sustain your Cart selections, keep you logged into your account, and enforce anti-tamper security.
  • Functional Preferences: Secure local browser tokens that store your selected currency, applied promo codes, and display parameters.

We do not utilize invasive cross-site advertising trackers or third-party behavioral spyware. You may configure your browser to decline cookies, though essential site features such as the Cart and checkout will not function without them.

8. Data Security & Retention Safeguards

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational security controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All transmissions across oddperfume.com are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit TLS (Transport Layer Security) cryptographic protocols.
  • Client passwords are encrypted with salted one-way cryptographic algorithms and are inaccessible even to our internal engineering team.
  • Staff access to customer records is restricted by role-based access control (RBAC) and dual-factor authentication.

Retention Period: We retain personal data strictly for as long as necessary to fulfill your purchases, service your account, and comply with statutory Indian tax (GST), legal accounting, and dispute resolution mandates (typically 7 years for financial records). When data is no longer required, it is securely destroyed or permanently anonymized.

9. Your Rights under the Indian DPDP Act, 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Indian consumer protection regulations, you hold enforceable rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: You may request the rectification of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete profile details.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your account and personal data, subject to statutory retention obligations under Indian taxation and corporate laws.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal mechanisms regarding our data practices.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to designate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, may exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our Grievance Officer at grievance@oddperfume.com from your registered email address.
